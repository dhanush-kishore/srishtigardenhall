Catering FAQs

Is the hall free if we book your catering?

Yes, always. Whenever you choose Srishti Caterers for more than 500 guests, the hall at Srishti Garden is free - it's our standard practice, not a limited offer. Call or WhatsApp +91 99454 20100 to check your date.

Do you serve both veg and non-veg food?

Yes. Srishti Caterers prepare both veg and non-veg menus.

Can you cater at another venue or at home?

Yes. Along with in-house catering at Srishti Garden Hall, we offer outdoor catering at your venue.

What kinds of events do you cater?

Weddings, receptions, engagements, baby showers, corporate events, and family functions.

How do I book catering or ask about menus?

Call or WhatsApp +91 99454 20100 with your event date and approximate guest count, and we'll talk you through menu options.
