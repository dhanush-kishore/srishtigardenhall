Catering by Srishti Caterers

VEGNON VEG

Srishti Caterers is the in-house catering team of Srishti Garden and Multipurpose Hall in Alangar, Moodbidri. We cook veg and non-veg meals for functions at our hall, and bring the same food to homes and other venues through our outdoor catering.

In-house catering

Food prepared in our own kitchen for every function at Srishti Garden Hall - two banquet halls seating up to 1,000 guests each.

Outdoor catering

Hosting at home or at another venue? Srishti Caterers can cater your event there.

Catering FAQs

Do you serve both veg and non-veg food?

Yes. Srishti Caterers prepare both veg and non-veg menus.

Yes. Along with in-house catering at Srishti Garden Hall, we offer outdoor catering at your venue.

What kinds of events do you cater?

Weddings, receptions, engagements, baby showers, corporate events, and family functions.
