The Venue

Room for everyone you love

From an intimate engagement to a 1,000-guest wedding, Srishti Garden gives you the space, the food, and the peace of mind to be fully present on your big day. Everything is on one site in Alangar, Moodbidri - your guests arrive easily, park easily, and leave well fed.

2 grand banquet halls

Up to 1,000 guests in each. Hold the ceremony in one and dinner in the other, or keep veg and non-veg dining on separate floors.

Parking for everyone

Full power back-up

Back-up for the whole venue. The lights and music keep going, whatever the weather does.
